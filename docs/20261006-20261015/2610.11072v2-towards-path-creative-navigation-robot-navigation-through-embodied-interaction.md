## 摘要
在杂乱且受限环境中的自主导航通常假设环境固定，并仅在现有自由空间内搜索路径。然而，一条路线可能被铰接结构、可移动物体或行人阻挡，因此到达目标可能需要与环境进行适当的具身交互。本文提出了路径创造式导航（Path-Creative Navigation, PCN），这是一种机器人通过协调运动和具身交互来恢复自由空间的导航范式，并利用无地图框架解决了一类 PCN 任务。该视觉-激光雷达-里程计框架通过统一的导航系统集成局部观测、障碍物几何信息和机器人状态估计，为导航与交互提供稳定的语义和几何信息。可通行性感知决策方法利用视觉和激光雷达距离信息来判断阻塞是否可操作以及是否可安全绕过，使机器人仅在必要时进行交互，同时支持推动铰接结构、推动可移动物体、避障以及向行人发出请求。我们设计了仿真场景，并在仿真和两个真实世界任务中评估了不同方法；这些真实任务若没有具身交互，仅靠传统导航无法完成。这些结果表明，我们的框架通过在杂乱且受限环境中协调导航与交互，相比基线方法实现了更高的任务完成率。

## Abstract
Autonomous navigation in cluttered and constrained environments typically assumes a fixed environment and searches only for paths within existing free space. However, a route can be blocked by an articulated structure, a movable object, or a pedestrian, and reaching the goal may therefore require appropriate embodied interaction with the environment. This paper formulates Path-Creative Navigation (PCN), a navigation paradigm in which the robot recovers free space by coordinating locomotion and embodied interaction, and addresses one class of PCN tasks with a mapless framework. The vision-LiDAR-odometry framework integrates local observations, obstacle geometry, and robot-state estimates through a unified navigation system, providing stable semantic and geometric information for navigation and interaction. The traversability-aware decision method utilizes visual and LiDAR distance information to determine whether a blockage is actionable and whether it can be safely bypassed, enabling the robot to interact only when necessary while supporting articulated structure pushing, movable object pushing, obstacle avoidance, and pedestrian requests. We design simulation scenarios and evaluate different methods in both simulation and two real-world tasks that cannot be completed by conventional navigation alone without embodied interaction. These results demonstrate that our framework achieves higher task completion than the baseline methods by coordinating navigation and interaction in cluttered and constrained environments.
