## 摘要
视觉语言模型（VLM）快速集成到敏感系统中，带来了现有研究尚未探索的关键安全漏洞。尽管对抗攻击鲁棒性已在基于图像的模型中得到广泛研究，但 VLM 在驾驶场景中针对视频的时序感知对抗攻击面前的脆弱性，构成了一种独特且尚未被充分研究的威胁。本文提出了一种针对用于自动驾驶场景的 VLM 模型的新型视频对抗攻击，称为时空一致性对抗攻击（STCA）。我们的攻击由三个阶段组成：模态扩展、空间攻击和 STCA 攻击。在模态扩展阶段，我们提出一种字幕引导的帧选择方法，以确保对抗扰动针对语义上最重要的帧。其次，在空间攻击阶段，我们构造有效的扰动并保持高相似度。然后，将生成的扰动视频输入 STCA 阶段，该阶段利用运动引导掩码破坏跨帧时序一致性。我们的方法在针对受害目标 VLM 的黑盒威胁模型下运行，仅依赖于白盒替代模型的可迁移性。我们在 BDD100K 和 nuScenes 自动驾驶数据集上，针对三个 VLM 模型进行了实验：Video LLaVA-7B、Qwen2.5-VL-7B 和 Dolphin。实验结果表明，空间攻击在保持高 SSIM 的同时实现了 ASR。我们的发现揭示，现有视频语言模型在自动驾驶场景中仍然极易受到对抗攻击，凸显了对 VLM 模型进行鲁棒防御的迫切需求。

## Abstract
The rapid integration of Vision Language Models (VLMs) into sensitive systems introduces critical safety vulnerabilities that remain unexplored in exist studies. While adversarial attack robustness has been extensively studied for image-based models, the susceptibility of VLMs to temporally-aware adversarial attacks against video in driving context poses a distinct and under examined threat. In this paper, we introduce novel adversarial attack against video targeting VLM models used for autonomous driving scenes named Spatial Temporal Coherence Adversarial Attack (STCA). Our attack comprise from three stages: modalities expansion, Spatial attack, and STCA attack. In modalities expansion, we propose caption-guided frame selection method in order to ensure that adversarial perturbation target the most semantically significant frames. Secondly.In spatial attack, we craft effective perturbation and preserve high similarity. Then the perturbed video generated fed into STCA stage that disrupt cross-frame temporal coherence using motion guided mask. Our method operate under black box threat model against victim target VLMs, relying solely on transferability from white-box surrogate model.We conduct our experiments on the BDD100K and nuScenes autonomous driving datasets across three VLM models: Video LLaVA-7B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, and Dolphin. Experimental results demonstrate spatial attack achieves an ASR with high SSIM. Our finding reveal that existing video language model, remain highly susceptible to adversarial attack in autonomous driving scenarios, underscoring the urgent need for robust defense for VLM models.
