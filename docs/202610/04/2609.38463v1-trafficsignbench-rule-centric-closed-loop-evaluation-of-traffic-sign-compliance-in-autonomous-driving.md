## 摘要
自动驾驶规划器通常使用驾驶得分、到达目的地率和碰撞率等聚合指标进行评估，这些指标并未明确衡量对交通规则的遵守情况。因此，规划器可以在基准测试中获得高分，同时仍表现出不安全或违规行为，限制了其在实际部署中的适用性。为弥补这一不足，我们提出 TrafficSignBench，一个大规模、以交通标志为中心的基准，用于系统且可解释地评估自动驾驶中的交通规则合规性。我们的框架将基于真实地图的仿真（用于生成逼真道路布局）与面向规则的程序化场景生成相结合，以可扩展且均衡地覆盖代表性不足的规则。我们实现了与 34 种交通标志相对应的交通规则，每个规则均配备自动规则检查器，用于在闭环执行过程中检测违规行为。该设计产生 29,000 个多样化道路场景和 29 种不同的测试场景类型，从而能够对特定规则下的规划器行为进行受控评估。我们构建了 5,800 个测试场景，并证明当前自动驾驶规划器尽管在标准评估指标上表现强劲，却可能表现出较差的交通规则合规性。为解决这一局限，我们通过显式交通标志约束将现有规划器转化为合规轨迹专家，从而可扩展地生成高质量 oracle 轨迹用于微调。

## Abstract
Autonomous driving planners are typically evaluated using aggregate metrics such as driving score, destination rate, and collision rate, which do not explicitly measure compliance with traffic rules. As a result, planners can achieve high benchmark scores while still exhibiting unsafe or illegal behaviors, limiting their applicability to real-world deployment. To address this gap, we introduce TrafficSignBench, a large-scale, traffic sign-centric benchmark for systematic and interpretable evaluation of traffic-rule compliance in autonomous driving. Our framework combines real-map-based simulation for realistic road layouts with rule-targeted procedural scenario generation for scalable and balanced coverage of underrepresented rules. We implement traffic rules corresponding to 34 traffic signs, each equipped with an automatic rule checker for detecting violations during closed-loop execution. This design yields 29,000 diverse road scenes and 29 distinct testing scenario types, enabling controlled evaluation of rule-specific planner behavior. We construct 5,800 testing scenes and demonstrate that current autonomous driving planners can exhibit poor traffic-rule compliance despite strong performance on standard evaluation metrics. To address this limitation, we transform existing planners into rule-compliant trajectory experts via explicit traffic-sign constraints, enabling scalable generation of high-quality oracle trajectories for fine-tuning.
