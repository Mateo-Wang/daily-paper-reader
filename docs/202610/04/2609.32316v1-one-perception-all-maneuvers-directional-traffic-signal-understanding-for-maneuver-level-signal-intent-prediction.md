## 摘要
交通灯是自动驾驶车辆在城市交叉口的关键监管信号，然而现有的交通信号感知仍主要被建模为实例级检测或颜色识别。这类建模能够识别交通灯的位置及其显示颜色，但在下游规划之前留下了关键的语义鸿沟：每个可见信号控制的是自车的哪一种机动，以及该信号对该机动表达了何种动态通行许可。本文提出方向性交通信号理解（Directional Traffic Signal Understanding），这是一项面向决策的任务，能够从前视图像中预测直行、左转、右转和掉头机动对应的结构化信号状态。每个状态包含相关联的信号颜色以及信号所暗示的可通行性。基于 OpenLane-V2，我们提供了一个方向级基准，包含机动级监督，以及针对颜色识别、可通行性、全帧一致性和安全关键错误的评价指标。一个方向感知的基线方法融合了全局上下文、局部交通灯证据以及机动特定表示。实验表明，与图像级分类器和面向检测的流程相比，方向级建模能够提升可通行性预测，尤其是在复杂的多信号交叉口场景中。由此得到的表示与拓扑、路线及周围智能体信息一起，为下游规划提供了直接且可解释的交通信号接口。

## Abstract
Traffic lights are a key regulatory signal for autonomous driving at urban intersections, yet existing traffic signal perception is still predominantly formulated as instance-level detection or color recognition. Such formulations identify where traffic lights are and what colors they display, but leave a critical semantic gap before downstream planning: which ego maneuver is controlled by each visible signal and what dynamic permission the signal expresses for that maneuver.   In this paper, we formulate Directional Traffic Signal Understanding, a decision-oriented task that predicts structured signal states for straight, left-turn, right-turn, and U-turn maneuvers from a front-view image. Each state contains the associated signal color and signal-implied passability. Based on OpenLane-V2, we provide a direction-level benchmark with maneuver-level supervision and metrics for color recognition, passability, full-frame consistency, and safety-critical errors. A direction-aware baseline combines global context, localized traffic-light evidence, and maneuver-specific representations. Experiments show that direction-level modeling improves passability prediction over image-level classifiers and detection-oriented pipelines, particularly at complex multi-signal intersections. The resulting representation provides a direct and interpretable traffic-signal interface for downstream planning together with topology, route, and surrounding-agent information.
