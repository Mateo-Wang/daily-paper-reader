## 摘要
模型预测控制（MPC）为决策提供了一种结构化且约束感知的机制，但其依赖便于优化的解析动力学模型，这限制了其在涉及接触以及其他难以建模的状态依赖任务中的应用。无模型强化学习避免了显式建模假设，但通常需要大量交互数据。我们提出了一种基于学习的MPC框架，它将局部基于模型规划的数据效率与结构同学习组件相结合，以补偿不完整动力学和有限时域短视。该方法用一个残差动力学网络增强标称解析模型，该网络从数据中学习缺失的状态依赖效应，并将由此得到的规划器与学习到的动作价值评论家相结合，后者将长时域MDP结构注入局部iLQR优化中。为使其在强化学习规模下切实可行，我们开发了一种GPU加速的批处理iLQR求解器，该求解器在最优控制循环内评估学习到的动力学和评论家网络，并并行求解数千个轨迹优化问题。完整系统被集成到机器人仿真器中，从而支持不完整动力学下的可扩展基于模型强化学习。在有偏和不完整建模的控制任务上的实验表明，该方法提高了闭环控制性能，同时保留了高效约束轨迹优化所需的基于模型结构。

## Abstract
Model Predictive Control (MPC) provides a structured and constraint-aware mechanism for decision-making, but its reliance on optimization-friendly analytical dynamics models limits its use in tasks with contacts and other hard-to-model state dependencies. Model-free reinforcement learning avoids explicit modeling assumptions but typically requires large amounts of interaction data. We present a learning-based MPC framework that combines the data efficiency and structure of local model-based planning with learned components that compensate for incomplete dynamics and finite-horizon myopia. The method augments a nominal analytical model with a residual dynamics network that learns missing state-dependent effects from data and combines the resulting planner with a learned action-value critic that injects long-horizon MDP structure into the local iLQR optimization. To make this practical at reinforcement-learning scale, we develop a GPU-accelerated batched iLQR solver that evaluates learned dynamics and critic networks inside the optimal-control loop and solves thousands of trajectory-optimization problems in parallel. The complete system is integrated into a robotics simulator, enabling scalable model-based reinforcement learning under incomplete dynamics. Experiments on biased and incompletely modeled control tasks show that the approach improves closed-loop control performance while preserving the model-based structure needed for efficient constrained trajectory optimization.
